Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2025

This Privacy Policy describes how Website Downloader ("we", "our", "us"), accessible at https://html-get.netlify.app/, collects, uses, and handles information when you use our service.

1. Information We Collect

Usage Data

When you use our service, our hosting providers (Netlify for the frontend, Render for the backend API) may automatically collect standard server log data including your IP address, browser type, operating system, referring URLs, and timestamps. This is standard practice for all web services.

URLs You Submit

When you submit a URL for download, that URL is sent to our backend API for processing. We do not permanently log or store submitted URLs beyond what is necessary to complete your download request. URLs are not shared with third parties.

2. How We Use Information

  • To process your download requests and return the ZIP archive to you.
  • To monitor server health, uptime, and diagnose technical errors.
  • To prevent abuse of the service (e.g., rate limiting).

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties for marketing purposes.

3. Data Storage & Retention

Downloaded files are temporarily stored on our backend server only for the duration required to package and stream the ZIP to your browser. These temporary files are automatically deleted after delivery. We do not maintain a permanent archive of websites you download.

4. Cookies & Local Storage

Our website uses browser localStorage solely to remember your preferred colour theme (light or dark mode). We do not use cookies for tracking or analytics purposes.

5. Third-Party Services

We rely on the following third-party services:

  • Netlify — Frontend hosting. Subject to Netlify's Privacy Policy.
  • Render — Backend API hosting. Subject to Render's Privacy Policy.
  • Google Fonts — Typography. Subject to Google's Privacy Policy.
  • Font Awesome — Icons served via Cloudflare CDN.

6. Children's Privacy

Our service is not directed at children under 13 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will post the updated date at the top of this page. Continued use of the service after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

8. Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please reach out through the project repository or contact us via the About page.
